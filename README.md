**Electronics Sales Dashboard** – Power BI
Project overview

This project is an interactive Power BI report designed to explore electronics sales performance, product and country trends, and customer behaviour.

The report is organised into three pages so that users can move from a high-level overview to more detailed product, country and customer analysis.
Report pages
1. Overview
A high-level view of sales performance, including:
- Total sales and other headline sales metrics
- Sales trend by month and year
- Sales by country
- Quantity by product category
- Sales by product
- Interactive filters for order date, country and product category
- A sales gauge for an at-a-glance comparison
Business questions:
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which countries contribute most to sales?
- Which product categories and products account for the most quantity sold?
- How do the results change when the report is filtered by date, country or category?
2. Country and Product Details
A closer look at product and category performance across countries, including:
- Quantity by product name
- Quantity by product category and country
- Sales by product category
Business questions:
- Which products sell the highest quantities?
- Which categories perform well in different countries?
- Are there differences between product volume and sales value?
3. Customers and Growth
A customer-focused page with measures and visuals for:
- Average order value
- Average units per order
- Year-over-year growth
- Orders per customer by country
- Average customer score by country
- Sales year to date
- Total customers and customers with a score
Business questions:
- How much does the average customer order?
- How many units are customers buying per order?
- How are sales performing year to date?
- How does customer activity or customer score vary by country?
Skills and tools
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data modelling and report organisation
- DAX measures and aggregations
- Interactive slicers and cross-filtering
- KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, column charts, a donut chart, a map, a gauge and a matrix/table
- Data analysis and visual storytelling
Analytical approach
The goal is not just to display figures, but to use different views of the data to ask better business questions.

For example, sales value and quantity sold provide different perspectives. A product with a high sales quantity does not necessarily generate the highest sales value. Similarly, country-level results can help identify where performance differs, while customer measures add context to the sales picture.

The report can support further investigation, but the visuals alone do not establish the reasons behind a trend. Additional context would be needed before making business recommendations.
How to use the report
1. Open the .pbix file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Start on the Overview page.
3. Use the date, country and product-category slicers to explore the results.
4. Continue to Country and Product Details to investigate product and market differences.
5. Open Customers and Growth to explore customer-related measures and sales growth.
